### 2018 · Day one at MMCOE
**Information Technology, with Honors in Data Science**
It started back in school, making little games in Scratch. That curiosity walked me into Marathwada Mitra Mandal's College of Engineering to study IT, with no idea yet that data would become the thing I'd chase.
📷 2022-mmcoe-project-completion.jpg (or a first-year photo if you find one)

### 2018 to 2022 · The events person
**Hosting, organising, and raising the bar**
If there was an event on campus, there was a good chance I was on the mic or behind the scenes making it happen. As Vice Chair of ACM MMCOE I helped run hackathons with teams joining from across the country, including our first ever online edition when COVID shut everything down. With ACM and ISTE we ran workshops, bootcamps and mock job interviews so students got hands-on practice before the real thing.
*What it taught me: running an event is shipping a product with a hard deadline and a live audience.*

### 2019 · Hello, world (officially)
**First certifications in JavaScript and Python**
Microsoft's MTA certifications in JavaScript and Python. Small badges, but they were the first proof to myself that I could actually do this.

### 2020 · Smart India Hackathon finalist
**An AI career guide for school students**
Six of us, Team Psycool, built an Android app that suggests careers to school students based on their aptitude and personality, and it took us to the Grand Finale of one of India's biggest innovation competitions. We didn't just code it: we visited schools to run aptitude and psychometric tests ourselves, and sat with professional counsellors to map student traits to real career paths.
*Behind the scenes: an AI recommendation system on Android, built on data we collected on the ground.*
📷 2020-sih-team-psycool.jpg

### Jun to Aug 2021 · First taste of real data
**Data Science intern at CareerGuide.com**
Three months working with real data for a real product.

### Jun 2022 · Published
**Revenue prediction and donor segmentation for NGOs**
Our final-year project turned into a paper in the International Journal of Computer Applications: forecasting NGO revenue and grouping donors so the NGO could plan better.
*Behind the scenes: SARIMA for forecasting, RFM and K-Means for segmentation.*

### Jul 2022 · Dassault Systèmes Solutions Lab
**R&D Software Engineering Associate**
Straight out of college into an engineering simulation company. I started on the interface side, building web components for a platform used by hundreds of customers. The first thing that hit me was how much an enterprise runs on process. Every step exists to keep you two steps ahead, so you learn to do it right the first time.
*Behind the scenes: Lit, Polymer, TypeScript and Redux.*

### Dec 2022 · Pondicherry, solo
**My first solo trip and my first ever scuba dive**
I packed a bag, went to Pondicherry alone, and ended up underwater for the first time.
> Some fears can only be conquered by diving straight into them.
📷 2022-pondicherry-dive.jpg, 2022-pondicherry-boat.jpg

### 2022 to 2024 · Making it faster
**Rebuilding the front end**
Moved a legacy codebase to Redux and TypeScript and chased rendering performance until load times dropped by 50 to 75%. Customer adoption grew two to three times after the rebuild.

### Mar 2023 · Star Alumni, Spoken Tutorial at IIT Bombay
**Back on stage, this time for my college**
IIT Bombay's Spoken Tutorial programme named me a Star Alumni from MMCOE's 2021-22 batch, and I received it at VMCC, IIT Bombay, in front of their faculty. A nice full-circle moment: the same college that let me run its events was now cheering from the other side.
📷 2023-iit-bombay-spoken-tutorial.jpg

### H2 2023 · Genius Award
**For my work in the second half of 2023**
My first big recognition at Dassault, for work on Process Composer and Performance Study: better monitoring and debugging for simulation jobs, a clearer tree view of running activities, and one-click navigation to errors. That stretch threw me into the whole product lifecycle, from figuring out the problem to shipping something users actually needed, and the problem-solving was the part I loved most. Grateful to the managers and teammates who backed me through it.
📷 2023-genius-award.jpg

### Apr 2024 · R&D Software Engineering Specialist
**A promotion, and a bigger say in what we built**
More ownership, more responsibility, and a push to think like the product owner, not just the person writing the code. It also came with room to try new ideas.

### Sep 2024 · Back to studying, while working full time
**M.Tech in AI and ML at BITS Pilani**
Honestly, it was a lot. Two years of balancing a full-time job, academics and life, often on barely six hours of sleep. Some days I was just trying to keep all the balls in the air. Somewhere along the way I learnt to manage my time, set boundaries, and accept that being productive doesn't mean being available all the time.

### 2024 · Applause Annual Award
**Inclusive Simulation Process Automation**
Our team's mission that year: make simulation inclusive, accessible and open to people who aren't simulation experts. Through a lot of iterations we shipped 100+ features focused on usability, inclusivity and performance, and the team won the Applause Annual Award for it.
*What it taught me: a team that challenges ideas, not people, ships better work.*
📷 2024-applause-award.jpg

### 2024 · Manali and Bir
**Paragliding off one of Asia's highest take-off points**
Bir Billing, a few thousand metres up, and then nothing below my feet. Hell of an experience.
> Up in the sky, I realized how small I am in this vast universe, and how beautiful it is to simply be a part of it and experience it.


### Apr 2025 · Generative AI Engineer
**From web components to AI**
No single lightbulb moment. Machine learning had hooked me since college, and every project pulled me further in, so I went all in on building with AI: assistants grounded in company knowledge, retrieval, and agents that stop and ask a human before they act.
*Behind the scenes: RAG, LangChain, MCP, human-in-the-loop tool calling.*

### 2026 · GraphMind
**My thesis became a platform**
Give it documents, code, audio or a whole repository and it builds a knowledge graph you can explore and question. It runs from my own Mac and powers [abhisheklalwani.in/graphmind](/graphmind).
